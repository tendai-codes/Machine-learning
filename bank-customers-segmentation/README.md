# Bank Customers Segmentation

## Problem

This project analysed bank customer data to identify distinct customer segments that could support targeted marketing and customer relationship strategies.

## Objective

- Explore behavioural and demographic characteristics of bank customers
- Identify natural groupings within the customer dataset
- Apply clustering techniques to segment customers based on observed attributes
- Support interpretation of customer structure using quantitative comparisons

## Approach

- Inspected dataset structure and variable types before segmentation
- Scaled numerical variables to ensure clustering compatibility
- Applied clustering methods to group customers with similar characteristics
- Visualised segment distributions to examine differences between customer groups

## Key Findings

- Clustering produced multiple customer segments based on similarity across selected attributes
- Segment comparisons showed observable differences in customer behaviour patterns
- Normalisation of numerical variables supported stable clustering outcomes
- Visual inspection of clusters enabled structured interpretation of customer group characteristics
